5 Expert Tips for Maintaining Your Balayage

Sarah Jenkins  |  Oct 15, 2026

Balayage is a beautiful, low-maintenance hair color technique, but it still requires some love to keep it looking fresh. Here are my top 5 tips.

1. Use Sulfate-Free Shampoo

Sulfates can strip natural oils and color from your hair. Switching to a gentle, color-safe shampoo is the first step in prolonging your look.

2. Wash Less Often

Washing your hair every day can cause the toner to fade faster. Try to extend your wash cycle to every 2-3 days and use dry shampoo in between.

3. Deep Condition Weekly

Bleached hair needs moisture. A weekly hair mask will keep your ends healthy and prevent breakage, making your color look smoother and shinier.

"Hydration is key. A well-moisturized strand holds color significantly longer than dry, brittle hair."

Follow these simple steps and your balayage will look salon-fresh for months! Ready for a touch-up? Book an appointment today.
